Let’s make a song.

If you’re an artist, record label or music publisher looking for original material, book a songwriting session with me. My sessions are centred around manifesting great ideas with music and crafting memorable songs.

As an experienced guitar and piano player, I often record instruments and vocals during the sessions I’m in. Some ideas develop into larger recordings that get handed off to mix engineers.

I take a professional approach to every session. Songwriting contributions and publishing splits are discussed in advance. All my songs get registered with PRS, ASCAP, Sentric, PPL plus other global music rights organisations.
